# Cinny pricing FAQ

## Is Cinny free?

Yes. Cinny is free to use, and everything you need to track movies and TV series is free with no caps:

- Unlimited logging, ratings, reviews, watch history and watchlist
- 20 swipes per day in the swipe deck (resets at 03:00 local time)
- 5 custom lists with up to 1,000 titles each
- Where to watch on every title page
- Core personal stats, including hours watched and a watch heatmap
- 3 favourite titles on your profile

## What is Cinny Plus?

Cinny Plus (called "Cinny Premium" in some places in the app) is an optional paid upgrade on top of the free app. It includes:

- Unlimited swipes in the swipe deck
- Up to 100 custom lists, plus collaborative lists you can invite friends to
- My streaming services: pick the services you pay for, see them ranked first on title pages and filter your lists with "On my services"
- Every personal stat, e.g. year vs year, genre breakdown, rating distribution and most-watched actors and directors
- Profile customisation: a profile backdrop, custom posters on favourites and reviews, and a Plus badge
- No ads

## How much does Cinny Plus cost?

| Plan | Price |
|---|---|
| Monthly | $3.99 per month |
| Annual | $24.99 per year (about $2.08 per month, 48% less than paying monthly) |
| Lifetime | $79.99, paid once |

Lifetime includes everything in Plus for good, a "Founder" badge and early access to new Plus features.

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. The annual plan comes with a 7-day free trial. The monthly and lifetime plans do not have a trial.

## Where can I buy Cinny Plus?

Only in the Cinny app for iOS and Android, through the App Store or Google Play. You can't buy Cinny Plus on the website.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime. Because you pay through the App Store or Google Play, you cancel in your Apple or Google account's subscription settings, not in Cinny. Plus stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for.

## Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds follow their rules. Request a refund from Apple or Google directly.

## What happens to my data if my subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. Plus features are locked, but:

- Your lists stay and you can still edit them
- Collaborative lists pause, and editors become viewers
- Your backdrop, custom posters and badge are hidden but saved, and they come back if you subscribe again

## Will my price go up?

If you're subscribed when a price changes, you keep your current price for as long as your subscription stays active. If your subscription lapses and you subscribe again later, the current price applies.

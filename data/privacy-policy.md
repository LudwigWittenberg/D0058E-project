## 2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Cinny.

### 2.1 Account Information

When you create a Cinny account, we may collect:

* your name
* username
* email address
* authentication information
* profile picture, if you choose to provide one
* profile biography, if you choose to provide one
* information about your account settings and preferences

If you sign in using Google or Apple, we receive information made available through the authentication process in accordance with your settings with that provider.

If you create an account using an email address and password, authentication credentials are processed to authenticate and secure your account. We do not need access to your password in readable form.

### 2.2 Movie and Series Activity

When you use Cinny, we process information about how you interact with movies and series, including:

* movies and series you have watched
* when you indicate that you watched something
* your watchlist
* ratings
* reviews
* recommendation activity
* related interactions with Cinny

This information is used to provide Cinny's tracking, social, and recommendation features.

### 2.3 Profile and Social Information

Cinny includes social features.

Your username, name, biography, profile picture if provided, and certain profile statistics, such as the number of movies and series you have watched, may be visible to other users.

Accounts are public by default. If your profile is public, other people may be able to see information such as your watched movies and series, watchlist, ratings, and reviews.

You can make your profile private. If your profile is private, users generally need to request to follow you and be accepted before they can see profile activity that is restricted to followers.

Reviews have separate visibility controls. Depending on the option you select, a review may be:

* private and visible only to you
* visible to approved followers
* public

You should not include sensitive personal information or information about other people in content you make public.

Information that you choose to make public may be viewed, copied, shared, or otherwise used by people outside your intended audience. Cinny cannot control what other people do with information after they have legitimately accessed it.

### 2.4 Recommendation Information

Cinny provides personalized movie and series recommendations.

Our recommendation system may use information associated with your Cinny user ID, including your watched movies and series and your watchlist, to determine recommendations that may be relevant to you.

Our recommendation system is developed and hosted by Codesquare AB.

Cinny may also provide explanations for recommendations, such as indicating that a movie or series is recommended because of something else you have watched.

We use machine learning techniques and embeddings as part of the recommendation system. We may use third-party artificial intelligence services to generate technical representations or embeddings of movie and series catalogue information.

We do not send Cinny user IDs, individual viewing histories, watchlists, names, or email addresses to those AI services for this purpose.

Personalized recommendations are intended to improve your experience. They do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning you.

### 2.5 Device and Technical Information

When you use Cinny, we may automatically receive technical information such as:

* device type
* operating system and app version
* IP address
* timezone
* technical identifiers
* push notification device tokens
* diagnostic information
* crash and error information
* information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service

Cinny uses a country or region setting to provide region-dependent functionality, such as indicating whether a movie or series is available in a particular country. That setting is taken from your device's language and region preferences when your account is created, and you can change it at any time in the app's settings.

Cinny does not use GPS or any other device location service, and never asks for location permission.

### 2.6 Analytics

If you choose to help us improve Cinny, we may collect product analytics about how you use the Service.

This may include information such as:

* screens or features used
* interactions with features
* app performance
* device and application information
* session and usage information
* crash and diagnostic information

We currently use services including PostHog, with EU-hosted infrastructure, and services provided through Expo to help operate, understand, maintain, and improve Cinny.

Where consent is legally required for optional analytics, we ask for that consent. You may withdraw your consent through the available settings.

On iOS, we ask for this permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and analytics is collected on an iOS device only if you allow it there. You can change this at any time under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device, or in Cinny's settings. On Android, we ask within the app.

Some technical processing may still be necessary regardless of your analytics preference where it is required to provide, secure, maintain, or troubleshoot the Service.

### 2.7 Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, we process a device push token and notification preferences so that we can send notifications to your device.

Depending on your settings, notifications may include:

* follow-related activity
* reminders to rate movies or series you have watched
* reminders relating to available Cinny features or interactions
* weekly reminders
* other notifications you choose to enable

You can manage notification categories through Cinny where available and can also disable notifications through your device settings.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing Cinny, maintaining security, complying with legal obligations, and resolving disputes.

Deleted information may remain temporarily in technical backups until those backups are overwritten or deleted as part of our normal backup processes. Information retained in backups is not intended to be restored for ordinary product use.

Different technical and diagnostic records may have shorter or longer retention periods depending on their purpose and the service provider involved.

## 12. Cookies and Web Technologies

Cinny's website may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies necessary for functionality, authentication, security, preferences, and, where enabled, analytics.

Where required by law, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies or similar tracking technologies.

We keep one aggregate count that does not depend on your choice: when a link or QR code on our website sends you onward to the App Store or Google Play, we record that a visit to that store listing happened.

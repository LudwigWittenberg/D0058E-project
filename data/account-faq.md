# Cinny account FAQ

## How do I sign in to Cinny?

You can sign in with email and password, Google, or Apple. If you sign up with email, you confirm your address with a 6-digit code. Every sign-in method that uses the same verified email address opens the same account.

## Can I use Cinny without an account?

Yes, you can try it first. Pick some genres and titles you love, and Cinny shows you recommendations right away. You can add them to your watchlist before creating an account.

## Can I change my username?

Yes, once every 30 days. A username is 3–24 characters and can contain lowercase letters, digits and underscores.

## How old do I have to be to use Cinny?

You must be 13 or older.

## How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account in the Cinny app or at cinny.app/delete-account. On the website you don't need to sign in, and you don't need to still have the app. You confirm the deletion through a single-use link sent to your email. The link expires after 5 minutes.

## How long until my data is deleted?

Your account and everything in it, such as your watchlist, history, ratings, reviews and lists, is deleted immediately when you confirm. There is no waiting period.

The only exceptions are information we are required to keep by law or for security and fraud prevention. Deleted data can also remain for a short time in technical backups until those backups are overwritten as part of our normal backup routine. Backups are never used to restore data for normal use of the app.

## Can I undo an account deletion?

No. Deletion happens immediately and completely, so a deleted account can't be restored.

## Does deleting my account cancel Cinny Plus?

No. Cinny Plus is billed through the App Store or Google Play, so cancel your subscription in your Apple or Google account's subscription settings before you delete your account.

## Can I export my watch history or other data?

There is no self-service export in the app yet; we are working on it. Until then, email info@codesquare.se and we'll help you get a copy of your data.

## Is my profile public?

Profiles are public by default. You can make your profile private so that only followers you approve can see it. Each diary entry and list can also be set to follow your profile setting, followers only, or private.

## Does Cinny sell my data?

No. Cinny does not sell personal data. Analytics are opt-in: nothing is collected until you agree, the data is never linked to your account, and you can change your answer in Settings at any time.

## Who is responsible for my data?

Cinny is operated by Codesquare AB in Sweden, which is the data controller under GDPR. For questions about your data or your privacy rights, email info@codesquare.se.

## I can't delete my account. What do I do?

Email info@codesquare.se and we'll help you.
